Privacy Policy - PiOS App - Gauteng, South Africa

revelationsa.co.za | Last Updated: 4 September 2026

1. Introduction

Revealed Logistics ("we", "us") operates revealed-logistics.vercel.app, a PiOS utility app for logistics services in South Africa. We are 100% Pi payments only.

2. Data We Collect

Via Pi SDK Only:

  • Pi Username (e.g. @username)
  • Pi User UID
  • Booking Details: Service Type, From, To, Date, Customer Name you enter

We DO NOT Collect: No email, no phone number, no ID, no bank account, no credit card, no fiat. All payments via Pi Blockchain.

3. How We Use Data

To fulfill your logistics booking (Tautliner, Reefer, Side Tipper, Fuel Tanker, Flatbed), confirm Pi payment, and provide delivery support in Gauteng, South Africa.

4. Pi Network Integration

We use Pi SDK: Pi.authenticate(['username','payments']) and Pi.createPayment(). Payments are approved via /api/approve-payment and completed via /api/complete-payment on Pi Testnet/Mainnet blockchain. We do not store Pi private keys.

5. Data Sharing

We share data only with Pi Network for payment verification. No third-party ads, no trackers, no selling of data.

6. Data Storage

Booking logs stored securely on Vercel serverless platform for 90 days for service fulfillment, then deleted. Pi payments stored on Pi blockchain permanently per Pi Network protocol.

7. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your booking data by emailing support@revelationsa.co.za with your Pi username.

8. Children's Privacy

App is for 18+ Pi Pioneers only, per Pi Network Terms.
